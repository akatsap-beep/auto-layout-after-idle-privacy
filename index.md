Privacy Policy for Auto Layout After Idle (ALAI)
Last updated: October 12, 2026

Auto Layout After Idle (ALAI) is a Windows desktop utility that changes the active keyboard layout after a user-selected period of keyboard inactivity.

Information collection
Auto Layout After Idle (ALAI) does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The application does not record, store, transmit, or analyze the content typed by the user. Keyboard activity is used only to determine when keyboard input last occurred. Typed characters and text are not retained.

Information stored locally
The application stores configuration and licensing information locally on the user's device. This information may include:

The selected keyboard layout
The configured inactivity period
Application preferences
Local trial-status information
An installed license key, when applicable
This information remains on the user's device and is not transmitted to the publisher or any third party.

Microsoft Store information
When Auto Layout After Idle (ALAI) is acquired through Microsoft Store, Microsoft may process purchase, licensing, diagnostic, and account information according to Microsoft's privacy terms.

Auto Layout After Idle (ALAI) does not independently receive or store Microsoft account passwords, payment-card details, or Microsoft Store payment information.

Internet access
The keyboard-layout functionality does not require an internet connection.

Microsoft Store may use an internet connection for installation, licensing, purchases, and application updates.

Third-party services
Auto Layout After Idle (ALAI) does not include advertising, analytics, tracking software, or third-party telemetry services.

Data retention and deletion
Application preferences, trial information, and licensing information are stored locally on the user's device.

Users can remove the application through Windows and may delete the AutoLayoutAfterIdle folder under the current user's local application-data location.

Children
Auto Layout After Idle (ALAI) is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed toward children. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy
This privacy policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be available on this page.
